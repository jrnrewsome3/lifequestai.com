The first class

Make AI useful in your work

A beginner-friendly, guided session built around a task you recognize from your own work.

Practice turning an idea into a useful draft, checking the result, and saving a template you can use again.

No coding required. Class length, delivery format, tools, and price will be confirmed with each group.

  1. 01

    Choose one useful task

    Identify a recurring task, describe what a good result looks like, and decide what information is appropriate to use.

  2. 02

    Give AI a clear brief

    Supply context, a specific request, constraints, and an example of the format you need.

  3. 03

    Review and improve the result

    Check facts, tone, missing information, and unsupported claims. Ask for a focused revision.

  4. 04

    Build your reusable template

    Save the prompt, the review checklist, and the steps you will repeat on your next task.

A ready-to-try prompt

Using only the fictional notes below, draft a friendly follow-up email under 120 words. Include one next step. Do not invent a date, price, or agreement. Use brackets for missing details.

Before you join

A few useful answers.

Is there a confirmed October class date?

October 2026 is our target launch window. We organize each class when 10 people with similar work needs are interested, then confirm a schedule with that group. A date is not guaranteed by joining the list.

What does it cost?

Pricing is not set yet. You’ll receive the price, date, duration, format, and tool requirements before deciding to enroll. Joining the interest list costs nothing and does not reserve a paid seat.

Will the class be online or in person?

Tell us your preference, location, time zone, and availability on the interest form. We’ll use that information when organizing the group.

Can colleagues join together?

Yes. Each person should join the interest list, or a team organizer can tell us how many people may be interested. We’ll confirm individual interest before counting an entire team toward a class.

What should I bring?

One task you want to improve, and fictional or non-sensitive examples you are permitted to use. We’ll confirm any account or software requirements in advance.

What happens to my information?

We use your contact details and work preferences to match a learning group and follow up about classes. This form does not sign you up for a newsletter. Please don’t include client, student, financial, or other confidential information.
