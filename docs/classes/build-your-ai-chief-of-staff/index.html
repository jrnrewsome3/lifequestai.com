Course overview

A Chief of Staff does not just answer questions — it watches what matters and tells you what needs attention. In this class you build a modular system: monitoring modules for email, calendar, documents and finances; preparation modules for meetings and research; and reporting modules that produce a daily brief and a weekly executive summary. You finish with your own blueprint and at least three working modules.

Who this is for

  • Executives, managers, and founders
  • Professionals with high coordination loads
  • Graduates of AI for Everyday Productivity
  • Anyone who ends each week feeling behind on inputs

What you’ll learn

  • The architecture of a modular assistant system
  • Email and calendar monitoring modules
  • Meeting preparation that runs before you ask
  • Document and file monitoring
  • Follow-up tracking that nothing falls out of
  • Research modules on standing topics
  • Financial and website activity monitoring
  • Daily briefings and weekly executive summaries
  • Designing your own Chief of Staff blueprint
