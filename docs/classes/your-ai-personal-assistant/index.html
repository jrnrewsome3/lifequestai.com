Course overview

This is where AI stops being a novelty and starts being useful every day. You will configure an assistant that knows your context, your preferences, and your recurring responsibilities — then put it to work on planning, writing, research, and decision support. The final project is your own personalized assistant.

Who this is for

  • People who use AI occasionally and want it to become a habit
  • Anyone juggling work, home, and personal projects
  • Graduates of Prompting for Real Life

What you’ll learn

  • Giving an assistant persistent context about you
  • Designing an assistant for a specific set of responsibilities
  • Planning, writing, and research workflows
  • Using AI for decision support without outsourcing the decision
  • Weekly and daily rhythms that keep it useful
  • Knowing when not to use your assistant

Curriculum

7 modules · 11 lessons
  • Auditing where your time actually goes

  • Projects, folders, and persistent context

  • Turning a messy brain dump into a plan
  • Weekly planning with AI
  • Breaking large projects into real next steps
  • Drafting in your own voice
  • Difficult messages and delicate replies
  • Editing rather than generating from scratch
  • Understanding a new subject quickly
  • Study aids and explanations at your level
  • Verifying what you learn
  • Structured comparisons and tradeoffs
  • Pressure-testing your own thinking
  • Where human judgment must stay in charge
  • Assembling your assistant’s instructions
  • Testing it against real tasks
  • Refining after a week of use

Write your personal context document
Configure a custom assistant and test it on five real tasks
Run one week using the assistant daily, then revise it
