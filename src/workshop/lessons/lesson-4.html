AI as Your Research Helper

AI can be a genuinely useful tool in your daily life — for health questions, planning, understanding documents, and learning. Here's how to use it wisely.

AI Is Like a Head Start, Not the Final Answer

Think of AI as a very smart starting point. It can explain a medical term in plain language, give you questions to ask your doctor, or summarize a long insurance document. But it should not be the last word on anything important.

The right approach: use AI to understand and prepare, then verify the important stuff with a professional — your doctor, your bank, or a trusted family member.

💡

The Two-Step Approach

Step 1: Use AI to understand and prepare — get a plain-language explanation, generate questions to ask, or get a summary.

Step 2: Verify anything important with a doctor, pharmacist, licensed professional, or trusted source like the Mayo Clinic or your bank's official website.

Real Ways AI Can Help You

🏥 Understanding Health Information

Your doctor uses medical language that can be confusing. AI can translate it into plain English.

"My doctor said I have 'atrial fibrillation.' Can you explain what that means in simple terms, and give me a list of questions I should ask at my next appointment?"

AI can give you a clear explanation and help you feel more prepared — not scared — going into your visit. Always bring any AI-provided information to your doctor and ask: "Is this accurate for my situation?"

Always verify: AI can be wrong about drug interactions, dosages, and individual health conditions. Use it to understand, then confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.

📄 Understanding Documents

Insurance policies, legal forms, Medicare notices — these documents are often written in confusing language. AI can help decode them.

"I just received a letter from Medicare that says 'Your claim for [service] has been denied under Coverage Determination.' Can you explain what this means and what my options are?"

You can even copy a section of a confusing document (but never include your personal ID numbers) and ask the AI to explain it in plain language.

Safety note: Never paste your Medicare number, Social Security number, or account numbers into AI. You can replace them with "XXXX" before pasting.

✈️ Planning Travel

AI is excellent at helping you plan a trip — suggesting destinations, creating packing lists, or finding accessible travel options.

"I'd like to take a 5-day trip to Asheville, North Carolina with my sister. We're both in our 70s and prefer relaxed sightseeing — no long hikes. What would you recommend, and what's the best time of year to visit?"

AI can suggest itineraries, local restaurants, accessible hotels, and things to consider. Always double-check reservations directly with hotels and airlines.

✍️ Writing Help

AI is very good at helping you write letters, emails, complaints, and even thank-you notes.

"Can you help me write a letter to my insurance company appealing a claim denial? The claim was for a specialist visit I needed, and the denial reason was 'not medically necessary.'"
"Help me write a firm but polite email to my landlord about a heating problem that hasn't been fixed after three requests."

You give AI the facts, and it helps you put them into clear, effective language. Always read the result carefully and make sure it says what you actually mean.

🌱 Learning Something New

AI is an incredibly patient teacher — it never makes you feel silly for asking a "basic" question.

"Can you teach me how to use Zoom for video calls with my grandchildren? Explain it step by step as if I've never done a video call before."
"What's the easiest way to start growing tomatoes if I've never gardened before? I have a sunny balcony and some pots."

AI will walk you through anything, step by step, at whatever pace you need. You can ask it to slow down, repeat a step, or explain something differently — as many times as you like.

Using Obsidian to Keep Your Notes

Obsidian is a free note-taking app that works offline (your notes stay on your own computer, not a company's server). It's great for keeping track of AI conversations, research, or anything you want to remember.

You can copy an AI response — like a summary of your health condition or a doctor's visit plan — and paste it into Obsidian to keep for later. Think of it like a personal notebook that lives on your computer.

📓

Why Obsidian is Good for Privacy

Unlike cloud-based notes apps, Obsidian stores your notes locally on your device. Your notes are not uploaded to any company's servers by default. This makes it a good choice for health notes or personal research.

When to Verify — Every Time

🏥

Health & medication

Always verify with your doctor or pharmacist before changing anything about your health or medications.

💰

Financial & legal

Tax advice, investment decisions, and legal questions — always consult a licensed professional. AI can help you understand, not decide.

📰

Current events

AI knowledge has a cutoff date — it may not know about recent news. For anything time-sensitive, check a news source you trust.

📋

Any "official" information

For Social Security, Medicare, or government benefits questions — always verify at the official government website (.gov addresses only).

✅

Safety Takeaway for This Lesson

  • AI is a great first step for research — not the final answer
  • Never paste personal ID numbers into AI — replace them with "XXXX"
  • Use AI to prepare questions for your doctor, not to replace your doctor
  • Always verify health, financial, and legal information with a professional
  • Obsidian is a private, local note-taking app — good for keeping your AI research organized

Great AI Prompts to Try

  • 💬 "Explain [medical term] in simple terms"
  • 💬 "Give me questions to ask my doctor about [condition]"
  • 💬 "Help me write a letter to [company] about [issue]"
  • 💬 "Summarize this paragraph in plain English"
  • 💬 "Plan a 3-day trip to [city] for two people"
  • 💬 "Teach me [skill] step by step, for a beginner"

Trusted Verify Sources

  • 🔗 Health: mayoclinic.org
  • 🔗 Health: nih.gov
  • 🔗 Medicare: medicare.gov
  • 🔗 Social Security: ssa.gov
  • 🔗 Tax info: irs.gov

Never Paste Into AI

  • ❌ Medicare / insurance ID numbers
  • ❌ Social Security numbers
  • ❌ Bank or credit card numbers
  • ❌ Full date of birth + address together
